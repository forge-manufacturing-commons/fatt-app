## Color-coding convention (used across all Nigeria schematic assets)
- **Forge Gold** — Assembly / Fabrication / Trade / general Manufacturing
- **Forge Cyan** — Precision / Automotive / Engineering Validation
- **Forge Emerald** — Heavy Industry / Solid Minerals / Agriculture Processing
- **Forge Heat Orange** — Energy / Oil & Gas / Foundry (hot processes)

Do not introduce a 5th color for a new industry category — assign it to the nearest existing bucket and note the mapping here.
