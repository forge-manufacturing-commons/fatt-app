# NMCP — NAWEDOAM Manufacturing Command Plate
## Specification · Rev A.02 · Forge Alpha Certified

The NMCP is the national manufacturing control surface of Forge OS — a
precision-manufactured industrial instrument, not a decorative map.

## Geometry
- Plate: 2.40 x 1.94 m, 35 mm forged steel, 10 deg presentation tilt
- Boundary: real GADM admin-1 data, dissolved to national outline, simplified to
  the 10 mm machining resolution (a chamfer cannot form on detail finer than itself)
- Gold chamfer: real geometry (not a shader), perimeter only
- Computed plate mass: 812 kg (plate volume x 7850 kg/m3 — CALCULATED, not weighed)

## Manufacturing hubs (18)
Each hub is a machined assembly: recessed pocket + LED emitter (below surface) +
gold retaining ring + crowned glass lens (IOR 1.52, clear coat). Coordinates are
real WGS84. Eight runtime states: Operating, Standby, Verification, Expansion,
Commissioning, Maintenance, Inspection, Offline.

## Network (readable without a legend)
- Power: recessed conduit, heat-oxide conductor seated inside
- Logistics: bright cream laser-engraved transport route
- Corridor: fine dark manufacturing datum line
- Inspection: cyan dashed verification ring

## Surface
Brushed steel base grain + procedural machining layer (radial facing passes,
cutter spokes) with low-frequency variation so operations read as non-uniform.
Bump peak 0.06 — machining, not scratches.

## Pedestal
Serviceable equipment base: 4x 80mm SHS legs, cross-bracing, cabinet, 2 access
panels, inspection hatch, equipment ID plate, 4 lifting points, power feed +
conduit run, 12-piece anchor set. 5 structural ribs under the plate (heavy-steel read).
